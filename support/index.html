How does Cleander work?

Swipe left to mark a photo or video for deletion, swipe right to keep it. Marked items wait in the trash until you review them. When you confirm, iOS asks once to delete them all.

I deleted photos, but my storage didn't free up

iOS moves deleted items to Recently Deleted and keeps them there for 30 days, so the space comes back only after that album is emptied. To free it now, open the Photos app, scroll down to Utilities, open Recently Deleted and delete the items there.

Cleander shows only some of my photos

Cleander has limited access to your library. To give it access to everything, open Settings › Apps › Cleander › Photos and choose Full Access.

Will deleting remove photos from my other devices?

If iCloud Photos is turned on, yes. A deletion applies to every device that uses the same Apple Account, and the items go to Recently Deleted there too.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Cleander › Cancel Subscription. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

I paid, but Pro isn't active

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. Then open the subscription screen in Cleander and tap Restore Purchases.

Does Cleander upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
