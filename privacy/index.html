Cleander

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Cleander is a photo cleaner for iPhone. It works entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any of your personal data.

Your photo library

With your permission, Cleander reads the photos and videos in your library to show them as cards, and deletes the ones you choose. Your photos never leave your device through Cleander.

  • Deleting uses the standard iOS confirmation. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, the Photos app may download originals from iCloud so Cleander can show them. This transfer happens between your device and Apple.
  • With iCloud Photos turned on, a deletion applies to every device that uses the same Apple Account.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, Cleander saves on your device:

  • which photos you chose to keep or delete (their identifiers, not the photos themselves);
  • whether you finished the introduction;
  • how many items you deleted today, for the free daily limit.

This data is part of your device backup like any app data, and it's removed when you delete the app.

What we don't do

Cleander has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. The app checks your subscription status on your device with Apple's StoreKit. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to purchases.

Children

Cleander isn't directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
